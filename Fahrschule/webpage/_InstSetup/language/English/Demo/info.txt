This is the Demo-Version of the Zusi-Train Simulator

To be used only for evaluation purposes.  Hosting on the Internet, 
Further Distribution, Public Performance and any use of the 
Data outside of the Demo is not permitted.

In the Demo, the following restrictions apply:

- Other Routes and Timetables are not selectable.
- Only the BR 216 and 120 are provided with realistic Driving Cab  
graphics.  The other vehicles use a symbolic Driving Cab.
- No Network Commmunication (Zusi-Display etc.)
- Limited access to Program Settings.
- No editing possible of the Routes, Vehicles and Timetables.
