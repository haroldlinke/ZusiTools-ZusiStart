Welcome to the Demo of the Zusi-Simulator

All commands needed to operate the Program can be accessed through the Menus above. To help get started, here are some direct links to the most important:

Driving School

As an introduction, Express Train "D 2640" is provided with a Driving School Mode. This provides a brief introduction and interactive help during the journey, covering all commonly encountered situations.

Start with Train Selection

One can also drive any of the other available Trains - these are without the support of the Driving School Assistant. Mark the desired Train by clicking in the List on the left and then click on the "Start" button beneath to begin.
Difficulty Level adjustment The Locomotive technology is such that in the beginning, one may spend more time stopped than actually driving! For an easier introduction some typical hurdles can be deactivated.
Graphic Settings Before the start of the 3D Graphics, the Graphic Mode (Windowed/Fullscreen etc.) may be selected in the Program Settings. Alterations to settings are possible during the Simulation ("End 3D-Process" > adjust Settings > "Resume Simulation").
Keyboard Assignments The Keyboard Assignents can be freely adjusted. Joysticks and the PI-Raildriver are also directly supported.
Documentation You would like a more detailed explanation of the Railway Technology and the capablities of the Simulator? The pdf-Documentation should answer all your questions.
