Quick Guide

With ZusiStart you can quickly and conveniently select and start a train.

The timetable of the selected train is automatically optimized to reduce the waiting time until the train starts.

  • Select a timetable group/route on the left.
  • The available timetables are displayed at the top center.
  • Select the desired timetable. All trains available in this timetable will be shown.
  • You can filter the trains by train number or passed station.
  • The train composition and the stations passed by the selected train are shown below.
  • Click Drive selected train to start the chosen train.
  • Favorites can be managed via the favorites tab.
  • Further options can be found under "Start Options" and in the menu at the top left.
Tip: With the Zusi route map and Zusi database you can specifically search for timetables and trains.

Further help can be found in the ZusiStart manual.
